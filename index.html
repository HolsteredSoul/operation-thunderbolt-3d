Screen won’t turn?

On iPhone, open Control Center: swipe down from the top-right corner (swipe up from the bottom on a phone with a Home button). Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock — the padlock with a circular arrow. Return here and turn sideways.

Your battle stays paused while the phone is upright.

Play on your iPhone

To play now, turn your phone sideways and tap PLAY IN SAFARI below. Adding a Home Screen icon is optional.

Remove Safari’s browser bars

  1. In Safari’s toolbar, tap …, then Share. If you already see the Share button, tap it directly:
  2. Scroll down the Share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen. If missing, use Edit Actions at the bottom to add it.
  3. Leave Open as Web App on if shown, then tap Add.
  4. Go to your iPhone’s Home Screen. Tap the new Thunderbolt icon, turn sideways, then tap PLAY.

These are Safari buttons, outside the game. The game cannot add the icon for you. If this page opened inside another app, first open the game link in Safari.
